About the Team

The New York Jets were founded in 1959 (originally the Titans of New York) and began play in 1960 as a charter member of the American Football League (AFL). The franchise adopted the "Jets" name in the early 1960s and became part of the National Football League after the AFL–NFL merger in 1970.

Major achievements:

  • Won Super Bowl III (1969 season) — the Jets defeated the Baltimore Colts in a landmark upset after quarterback Joe Namath famously guaranteed victory.
  • AFL champions in 1968 and several division titles thereafter.

Key points:

  • The Jets have a passionate fan base in the New York metropolitan area and play their home games at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey (shared with the New York Giants).
  • Hall of Fame players and historic moments (including Joe Namath's guarantee) are a big part of the team's legacy.
  • The franchise has long-standing rivalries within the AFC East and remains a prominent part of pro football history in the region.
